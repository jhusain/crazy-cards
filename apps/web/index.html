House rules

  • Tap a card to queue it. Flick a matching card up from your hand to play it immediately when it has no card that can follow it. Tap the discard pile or flick any queued card up to play the queue. Flick any queued card down to clear the queue.
  • Eights are wild. A single eight must call a different suit. With multiple eights, the last eight can call any suit.
  • Aces and jacks skip the opponent. Twos make them draw two per card in the stack, and the queen of spades makes them draw five. A special effect can be covered by an ordinary card in the combo. After an ace or jack, you can queue another play that matches the suit of the last card.
  • Tap the table or deck to clear a queue or draw. After drawing, tap the table or either pile to pass. The table draws for you when you have no legal play and passes if you still can’t play.
  • On your turn, tap Instant Replay to see the last sequence at half speed, including your draw and current queue. Tap anywhere to return to the game.
  • Going out only wins when your turn comes back. If you both go out, the round is a tie. The winner gains one point for each card the other player has left, and the loser gives up that many points.
