Sound and vibration

Enable countdown ticks, impact sounds, and wild-card suit chimes? Aggressive plays vibrate whether sound is on or off. Countdowns vibrate when sound is off. You can change sound in Settings.

House rules

  • Add ?debug=true to the URL, or double-tap the opponent’s cards, to toggle debug mode. In debug mode, tap an empty discard play to let the AI take your turn, or enable Autoplay in the menu to control your seat automatically. Opening the menu pauses autoplay.
  • Tap a card to add it to your play. Flick a matching card up to play its rank group with that card on the bottom, choosing the top suit when needed. Enable “Flick selects top card instead of bottom card” to select the top card instead; in that mode, flicking the only matching card plays it individually. In Settings you can stage a flicked eight or two when others of the same rank remain in your hand. Tap the discard pile or swipe any queued card up or left to play the queue. Flick any queued card down to clear the queue.
  • Eights are wild. A single eight must call a different suit. With multiple eights, the last eight can call any suit.
  • Your queued play sits to the right of the last play, separated by a gap. Tap the last play or swipe a queued card up or left to play it. Tap a queued card to return it to your hand; flick down to clear the queue.
  • Aces and jacks skip the opponent. Twos make them draw two per card in the stack, and the queen of spades makes them draw five. A special effect can be covered by an ordinary card in the combo. After an ace or jack, you can queue another play that matches the suit of the last card.
  • Tap the deck to clear a queue or draw. After drawing, tap either pile to pass. Tapping the table does nothing. The table draws for you when you have no legal play and passes if you still can’t play.
  • On your turn, with no cards queued, tap the Instant Replay icon to see the last sequence at half speed, including forced draws and passes when you could not play. Tap anywhere or use the fast-forward icon to return to the game.
  • Going out only wins when your turn comes back. If you both go out, the round is a tie. The deck shows the points per card: reshuffling the discard doubles it. A tie doubles the next round’s starting value; a decisive round resets the next round’s starting value to one. The winner gains the current points per card for each opposing card left, and the loser gives up the same number. A match ends after three decisive rounds; the higher total score wins.
